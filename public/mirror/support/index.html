Support

Help with Everyday Mirror. Contact alex@ajwoo.com. Publisher: GG Creative Studios, LLC.

Camera or flashlight

Allow Camera access in iOS Settings. If the preview freezes, close and reopen the app. Flashlight support depends on your device and camera state. If a problem continues, send your device model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce it.

Restore Pro

Open Settings → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that purchased Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase. Full reset switches this device to free mode but keeps the Apple purchase available to restore.

Save, share and reset

Allow Photos access to save captures. Sharing uses the destination you select. Full reset clears local preferences, usage history and temporary captures; it does not delete saved Photos, cancel a purchase or reset iOS permissions or advertising consent.

Privacy, refunds and security

Read our Privacy policy and Terms. Request purchase refunds through https://reportaproblem.apple.com/. Send privacy requests or security reports to alex@ajwoo.com. Please avoid including private captures or passwords.
