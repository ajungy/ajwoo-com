Privacy policy

Effective September 22, 2026. This policy covers Everyday Mirror for iOS, provided by GG Creative Studios, LLC (“we”, “us”). Contact alex@ajwoo.com for privacy questions and requests.

Camera and captures

Camera frames are processed on your device to display the mirror and adjust brightness. We do not receive your camera feed, photos or videos. We do not perform facial identification or create biometric identity templates. Camera permission is necessary for the mirror. Saving requires add-only Photos permission; the app does not read your photo library. Sharing sends the file to the service or person you select, subject to that service’s practices.

Local information

Preferences, a count of app openings used for Apple’s rating prompt, and Pro access state are stored on your device. We do not operate an app account or a server holding these records. Temporary captures are removed when dismissed or reset, or cleaned on a later app launch after 24 hours. Saved Photos remain until you delete them. Your device backup settings may retain local app data.

Advertising

Free mode integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. When ads are requested, Google may process IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate its services. We request non-personalized ads. This does not mean no personal data is processed. We do not request permission for cross-app tracking or obtain the advertising identifier through our own code. Everyday Mirror Pro removes banner ads; upgrading does not erase information already processed by advertising providers. We do not send camera images or captures to AdMob.

Purchases and support

Apple processes payments and purchase history. The app uses verified StoreKit purchase information to provide and restore Pro; we do not receive your payment card details. If you email us, we receive your address, message, and attachments through our email provider. Please do not send sensitive images or unnecessary personal information. We use correspondence to answer requests, resolve problems, protect our services and meet legal obligations.

Purposes and legal bases

Where European, UK or similar law requires a legal basis, we rely on performing our contract for requested functionality and purchase support; consent for advertising-related storage or processing where required; legitimate interests in responding to support, security and fraud prevention where these do not override your rights; and legal obligations for required records and lawful requests. You may withdraw consent through available advertising privacy choices without affecting processing already lawfully performed. Refusing optional advertising consent does not prevent use of the mirror.

Sharing and international processing

Apple, Google, email providers and services you choose to share with process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We may disclose support records when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights, and to advisers under confidentiality obligations. Our business is in the United States; providers may process information in other countries. Where applicable, transfers require a recognized legal mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about safeguards applicable to information we control. Google and Apple describe their own transfers and safeguards in their policies.

Retention and security

We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and for up to 24 months after closure, unless a dispute, legal hold or legal obligation requires longer retention. We then delete or anonymize it; provider backups may take additional time to expire. Local retention is described above. Google and Apple determine retention for information they independently control. We use reasonable safeguards and limit access to support information; no system or transmission is guaranteed secure.

Your choices and rights

Change camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Use Full reset to clear local settings and temporary captures, or remove the app to remove its local data; saved Photos, backups, Apple purchases and advertising consent are separate. Advertising privacy choices appear in app Settings when required by the consent provider. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a portable copy, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or an appeal of a denied request. Email alex@ajwoo.com with the request and your region. We may seek proportionate identity verification and will respond within applicable deadlines. You may use an authorized agent where the law permits, and complain to your local supervisory authority. We will not discriminate for exercising protected rights.

U.S. state privacy choices

We do not sell camera images or support messages. Advertising disclosures can be treated as a sale, sharing or targeted advertising under some state laws even without payment for data. Where applicable, use Advertising privacy choices or contact us to opt out and exercise state privacy rights. We do not use camera content for advertising and do not knowingly sell or share information of children under 16. We do not collect precise location through an app permission. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied information should contact us for deletion. An App Store content rating is not a statement that advertising data collection is intended for children.

Changes and provider policies

We will update this date when the policy changes, and provide additional notice or request consent when required. Google privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Apple privacy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Policy website

Our support and policy website has no advertising, analytics scripts or contact form added by us. Its hosting service necessarily receives technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the pages. Contact us about privacy requests relating to this site.
