ajwoo capture — App privacy notice
September 13, 2026 | GG Creative Studios, LLC | alex@ajwoo.com

Scope: the installed Capture app. Website hosting, download requests, embedded
videos and any future checkout are separate services and are not covered by a
claim that the app works locally.

On your Mac
Screen, camera and microphone recordings, cursor positions/clicks, editing data,
transcripts and generated caches are stored locally. Recording packages are saved
under ~/Movies/ajwoo capture. Imported background images and app settings may also
be stored in your Library/Application Support and preferences folders. You control
these files and can delete them. Keep backups before deleting recording packages.
Favorites must be unfavorited before the app's delete action will remove them.

Recording permissions
macOS asks for Screen Recording access and, when enabled, camera and microphone
access. You can change these in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Disabling a
permission prevents the corresponding capture feature from working.

Connections and speech
The app has no account system, built-in analytics or automatic crash-upload service.
It does not upload recordings or transcripts to the publisher. Transcription uses
Apple's on-device speech APIs; Apple may download required language/model resources
if they are missing. Apple controls the delivery of those resources and macOS
security/signature checks. If on-device recognition is unavailable, the feature may
not work. Exporting to a file or copying to your clipboard remains local until you
or another application shares that content.

If you contact us
Your email and anything you choose to include will be available to the publisher
and its email provider for responding to your request. Do not attach sensitive
recordings. Contact alex@ajwoo.com to request deletion of support correspondence;
necessary legal or security records may need to be retained.

Downloading from the website
The website/CDN receives connection information such as your IP address and browser
request information to deliver the site and ZIP. Embedded third-party videos can
make requests to their providers. This app privacy notice does not promise that the
website or operating system makes no network connections.
