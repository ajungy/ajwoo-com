INSTALL
Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14 or later.
Unzip the download, drag ajwoo capture.app to Applications, then open it.
Allow Screen Recording when asked. Enable camera and microphone only if needed.
Normal first-open and privacy prompts may appear. Keep macOS security enabled.

TEST BEFORE IMPORTANT WORK
This app is still in development and is offered for beta testing and evaluation,
including educational use. Recording, editing, transcription and export may have
errors, interruptions or compatibility issues. Keep separate backups of important
files. Make a short test recording and check the exported picture and sound before
relying on Capture for important work. Review automatic cuts and subtitles.
No guarantee of uninterrupted operation, perfect accuracy or zero data loss is made.
These notices do not limit rights or remedies that applicable law does not allow
us to limit. Beta or educational labeling is not a blanket exemption from liability.

RECORD RESPONSIBLY
Record only content you have permission to record. Obtain any legally required
consent from participants, and avoid exposing passwords, private messages or
confidential information. Review the whole export before sharing it.
